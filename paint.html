    Use your own art

    Bring a designer's drawings to life instead of painting.

    Got drawings from a designer? Add one image per pose and Critterkit's rig brings them to life. Transparent PNGs work best. Only Neutral is required: missing poses reuse it, and a single pointing pose is mirrored for the other side.

    What to ask your designer for
    • The same character, same size and position, on the same canvas size in every image.
    • Full body, facing the viewer, with a transparent background (or a plain one).
    • Neutral, Blink (eyes closed), Happy, Talking "ah" and "ee", Worried, Surprised, Confused.
    • Wave (one hand raised) and a second wave frame with the same hand a bit lower.
    • Pointing left and pointing right with the arm stretched out.
    • Name files after the pose (e.g. happy.png) and "Add several at once" sorts them for you.

    Add at least the Neutral pose.
