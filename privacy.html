Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Critterkit is a product of Zero to Build LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect when you use critterkit.com, the builder and painter, and the Critterkit code in apps and websites. We collect as little as we can and we don’t sell personal information.

What we collect

  • Email for updates. If you join the email list, we keep your email address, the time you signed up and which page you signed up from.
  • Purchases. Our payment provider handles your card details; we never see or store them. We receive the email address on the order and an order ID, and we store your license key, how many paints it has left and the orders linked to it.
  • What you create. The description you type, the style you choose, and any photo or artwork you upload are sent to create your mascot. When you save a mascot, we store the painting, its prepared poses, its name and its description.
  • Security data. To stop people guessing license keys, we record failed unlock attempts against a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for up to an hour. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for a short time.
  • Stored in your browser. The painter keeps your most recent painting and your license key in your own browser so they’re there when you come back. You can clear them by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • Visit statistics. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views and where visitors come from. These don’t use advertising cookies or track you across other sites.

People who see a Critterkit mascot in someone else’s app

When an app or website shows a Critterkit mascot, the visitor’s browser loads the mascot and our script from our servers, which means our hosting provider sees a normal web request (including IP address). We don’t use that to identify or track people, and the mascot doesn’t collect what they type or do in the app. The app’s owner is responsible for their own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To create, store and deliver your mascot.
  • To process purchases, issue license keys and track paints.
  • To send product updates and launch news if you joined the email list. You can ask us to remove you at any time.
  • To keep Critterkit secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services Critterkit runs on, and only what they need:

  • Vercel hosts the website, runs our servers and stores saved mascots, keys and email signups.
  • OpenAI receives your description and any uploaded photo or artwork to paint and check your mascot. Under OpenAI’s API terms, this data isn’t used to train their models by default.
  • Our payment provider (currently Polar, with Stripe processing cards) handles checkout as the seller of record, under its own privacy policy.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or other people’s safety. If Zero to Build LLC or Critterkit is sold or merged, information may transfer as part of that.

Saved mascots are public by link

Anyone with a saved mascot’s ID can load it, because that’s how it appears in your app. Don’t upload photos of people or anything private you wouldn’t want shown publicly.

How long we keep it

We keep saved mascots and license keys while Critterkit is running so your mascot keeps working. We keep email signups until you ask us to remove them. Security records of failed attempts expire after about an hour. Uploaded photos are used to create the painting; we don’t keep the original photo after that.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we have about you, or to remove you from the email list, by writing to hello@critterkit.com. Deleting a mascot means it will stop loading in any app that uses it. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have additional rights, and we’ll honor them.

Children

Critterkit is for app makers, not children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll make it clear on the site.
